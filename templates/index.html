①→④ How it works

1 Detect

YOLO finds every player in every frame of your video.

2 Fingerprint

ResNet turns each player crop into a unique visual vector.

3 Track

Fingerprints + positions link the same player across frames.

4 Render

Each player gets a stable colored box and ID number.
